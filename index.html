What we do

Our services

Every project is designed around your space, your style and your budget, and finished to a standard we're proud to put our name to.

01

Kitchens

We supply and fit new kitchens, or refit and update the one you have. Units, worktops, end panels and a clean finish all round. Includes a free basic CAD design.

02

Bespoke Wardrobes

Fitted and sliding-door wardrobes built to use every inch, including alcoves, sloped ceilings and awkward corners. Interior lighting is optional. Includes a free basic CAD design.

03

Media Walls

Slatted and panelled feature walls with a recessed TV, an electric fire, floating units, hidden cables and LED lighting.

04

Internal Doors

We supply and hang internal doors and fire doors, fit frames, linings, architrave and ironmongery.

05

External Doors

Secure, weather-tight composite and timber front and back doors, fitted with quality locks.

06

General Joinery

Skirting, architrave, shelving, boxing in, repairs, and the jobs that need a skilled pair of hands.

About us

Proper joinery, done right first time.

Get Knotted Joinery is run by Jason, a fully time-served joiner with over 20 years in the trade. Jason holds an HNC in Joinery and Carpentry and Level 2 & 3 qualifications from Motherwell College.

Whether it's a single door or a full kitchen, you deal directly with the person who measures, makes and fits your project, from the first visit to the final clean-up. We take pride in neat, solid work that looks right and lasts.

We work across Glasgow, Lanarkshire and Ayrshire, and take on projects in the North of England.
